Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This is my personal portfolio. I keep data collection to a minimum: no accounts, no forms, no ads. The only optional part is Google Analytics, which runs only if you accept it.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Fabrizio Pasini, Rome, Italy. For anything about your data, email pasini.fa@gmail.com.

Analytics (only with your consent)

If you click Accept on the cookie banner, the site loads Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited. If you decline, or ignore the banner, Google Analytics is not loaded at all.

  • What it collects: pages viewed, time on page, links clicked, the site that referred you, device type, browser, screen size, and approximate location (country or city) derived from your IP address. Google Analytics 4 does not store your full IP address.
  • Cookies set: _ga and _ga_<ID>, which hold a random identifier and expire after up to 2 years.
  • Why: to see which projects and pages people look at, so I can improve the portfolio.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code). You can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect processing that happened before.
  • Retention: analytics data is kept for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically by Google.

Your current choice: not set

Declining after you had accepted switches Google Analytics off and deletes its cookies from this site. You can also block cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out browser add-on.

What happens on every visit

Hosting — GitHub Pages

The site is hosted by GitHub, Inc. To deliver pages and keep the service secure, GitHub's servers process technical data such as your IP address, browser and the time of the request. Legal basis: legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Fonts and icons

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (Google) and icons from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Your browser requests these files directly, which shares your IP address and browser details with those providers. No cookies are set. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a consistent, fast-loading design (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Local storage

Your cookie choice is saved in your browser's local storage under analyticsConsent, so the banner doesn't come back on every page. It never leaves your device and is strictly necessary to remember your choice.

If you email me

If you write to me, I use your email address and message only to reply and to discuss the work you asked about. I keep the conversation for as long as it's useful for that, and never share it for marketing.

Links to other sites

Project and social links lead to sites such as itch.io, ArtStation, Behance, YouTube, LinkedIn, X and GitHub. Once you open them, their own privacy policies apply.

Transfers outside the EU

Google, GitHub and Cloudflare may process data in the United States. These transfers rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to:

  • access the personal data I hold about you, and get a copy;
  • correct it, delete it, or restrict how it's used;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

Email pasini.fa@gmail.com and I'll answer within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or with the authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
